# 🔴 Río Tinto Benchmark Deliverables (`data/output_3d/rio_tinto/`)

## Overview

This folder contains the complete scientific deliverable package generated for the **Río Tinto Fluvial Basin (Huelva, Andalusia, Spain)** evaluated as a positive terrestrial analog for **Meridiani Planum and Ancient Hydrated Martian Environments (Mars)**.

---

## Site Specifications

- **Location:** Iberian Pyrite Belt, Huelva, Andalusia, Spain
- **Centroid Coordinates:** `37.6970° N, 6.5560° W` (WGS84 / EPSG:4326)
- **Local Projection:** `EPSG:32629` (UTM Zone 29N)
- **Geochemistry & Mineralogy:** Extreme acidic fluvial system ($pH \approx 1.5 - 2.5$) with high concentrations of ferric iron ($Fe^{3+}$) and sulfates. Extensive deposits of jarosite, schwertmannite, and goethite precipitated through chemolithoautotrophic bio-oxidation of pyrite ($FeS_2$).
- **Martian Mission Pairing:** Meridiani Planum (Opportunity Rover landing site / jarosite outcrops) and Mawrth Vallis (Mars Express OMEGA / MRO CRISM phyllosilicate and sulfate exposures).
- **Core 100-Meter Radius Coordinates:** Centered over an active iron-terrace meander and gossan exposure.
